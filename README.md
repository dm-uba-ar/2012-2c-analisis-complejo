  to the theory of analytic functions of one complex
  variable.* Third edition. International Series in
  Pure and Applied Mathematics. McGraw-Hill.
- Cartan, Henri *Elementary theory of analytic functions of one or several complex
  variables.* Dover.
- Conway, John B. *Functions of one complex
  variable.* Second edition. Graduate Texts in
  Mathematics, 11. Springer-Verlag.
- Markushevich, A. I. *Theory of functions of a complex
  variable.* Vol. I. Prentice-Hall.
- Remmert, Reinhold. *Theory of complex
  functions.*
  Graduate Texts in Mathematics, 122. Readings in
  Mathematics. Springer-Verlag.
